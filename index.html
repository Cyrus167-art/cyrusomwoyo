Welcome...about Cyrus

An environmental scientist and DAAD alumnus (MSc Integrated Watershed Management, Kenyatta University). With over 10 years of serving in diverse roles in various organizations within the water–energy (biogas)–agriculture nexus, I have gained substantial knowledge and expertise in environmental management and climate action. Recently, I completed a high-level German Agency for International Cooperation (GIZ) consultancy as the Knowledge Management and Communications Advisor (Consultant) for the African Biodigester Component–Kenya, where my performance received positive evaluation. I am an experienced environmental/social researcher, proficient in qualitative and quantitative research and familiar with using modeling and analysis tools such as SPSS, Microsoft Excel, ArcGIS/QGIS, R, and HEC-HMS to support evidence-based decisions and conclusions. I am deeply interested in the application of technology, innovation, and data-driven approaches to address climate change, and I value analytical and systems-thinking approaches to problem-solving.
